Season record

Each week the model's five highest-rated spread plays are the only official plays this season (the backtest of past seasons also includes over/under plays). Only plays with a gap of 3 or more points qualify, so a week can have fewer than five. For this season's finished weeks, the plays shown are the top five spread plays the model would have made going into each week, built after the fact from its pre-game predictions at the final lines, so they replace the plays first logged live. Each play's side is fixed when it is logged before kickoff, then graded after the game. The line on a pending play follows the market until kickoff and then freezes, and the play is graded at that final line. Every change is saved in this site's public repository history (data/picks_nfl.json). Occasional manual corrections are in data/line_overrides.json.

OFFICIAL PLAYS

This week's plays

The model's five biggest gaps from the Vegas line, locked before kickoff and graded in public.

Each week the five spread plays where the model differs most from the Vegas line are the official plays (over/under plays are not official this season): 1.5 units when the gap is 4+ points, 1 unit for 3 to 4. The five plays and their sides lock about three days before the week's first kickoff. Each play's line then follows the market until its game kicks off. When the Vegas spread is within 2 points either way, the play is a moneyline pick on the side the model likes, paid at the posted moneyline. Every other game still shows the model's side below, but isn't an official play. Lines move, so check the current number before you bet.

About

Kickoff Labs rates teams with an Elo system plus recent efficiency (EPA per play), quarterback quality, injuries and weather, then projects each game's score and compares it with the Vegas lines. The model is less accurate than the betting market overall, so its differences from Vegas are most likely noise. Treat them as ideas to examine, not sure things.
