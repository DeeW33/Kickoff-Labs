About

Kickoff Labs rates teams with an Elo system plus recent efficiency (EPA per play), quarterback quality, injuries and weather, then projects each game's score and compares it with the Vegas lines. The model is less accurate than the betting market overall, so its differences from Vegas are most likely noise. Treat them as ideas to examine, not sure things.
