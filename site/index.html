The free odds feed can lag the market, so a line is occasionally corrected to the number that was actually available when the pick was posted. Every correction is recorded in this site's public repository history (data/line_overrides.json).

This week's official plays

Each week the five plays where the model differs most from the Vegas line are the official plays: 1.5 units when the gap is 4+ points, 1 unit for 3 to 4. They lock about three days before the week's first kickoff and don't change after that. When the Vegas spread is within 2 points either way, the play is a moneyline pick on the side the model likes, paid at the posted moneyline. About

Kickoff Labs rates teams with an Elo system plus recent efficiency (EPA per play), quarterback quality, injuries and weather, then projects each game's score and compares it with the Vegas lines. The model is less accurate than the betting market overall, so its differences from Vegas are most likely noise. Treat them as ideas to examine, not sure things.
